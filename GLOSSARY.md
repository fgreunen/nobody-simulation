# Stellar Simulation

A scene-based experience for observing stellar motion under gravity and making limited interventions.

## Language

**Scene**:
A starting arrangement of bodies and tracer particles, including their positions and motion.
_Avoid_: Level, match

**Gravitating body**:
An object whose mass contributes to the gravitational field and whose motion responds to gravity.
_Avoid_: Particle when referring specifically to a source of gravity

**Tracer particle**:
A visible object that responds to gravity without contributing its own gravitational influence.
_Avoid_: Gravitating body, decorative particle

**Intervention**:
A limited user action that alters an evolving scene, such as introducing a gravity well.
_Avoid_: Gameplay objective

**Preset**:
A configurable recipe for a scene's starting arrangement and motion.
_Avoid_: Level

**Gravity well**:
A fictional intervention that attracts nearby bodies and tracer particles.
_Avoid_: Black hole when referring to this intervention

**Reset**:
A return to the same initial scene, discarding its subsequent evolution.
_Avoid_: Rewind
